# Cover letter — Clinical Trial Manager and SR Clinical Trial Manager (ICON plc)

Dear Hiring Manager,

I currently hold that kind of accountability at portfolio scale. On the sponsor-facing side, I am Global Study Manager on HORIZON, the largest trial Novartis runs with Labcorp. I have represented Labcorp to Novartis in person in London, Basel and London again, I participate regularly in client audits, and I have taken part in four bid defenses. Building and keeping a sponsor's trust while a trial is under pressure is a skill I have been tested on.

Here is the difference I want to be upfront about. My experience is interventional: Oncology, Autoimmune and Malaria trials, with budgets above $20M, first as Global Clinical Study Manager and before that as Regional Study Coordinator for EMEA. The delivery mechanics in your posting, site lifecycle oversight, monitoring plans, CTMS discipline, risk mitigation off metrics and dashboards, are the same discipline, but I would be learning the study designs and their specific regulatory pathways on the job, and I would rather say so than let you find out.

I hold a Certificate in Project Management from Rutgers. I work in English and Portuguese at C2 level, with working German, which matters for a Brazil-based role on a global team.

Thank you for considering my application.
